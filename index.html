About this data

Each digest is a snapshot of what the AI subreddits were discussing when it ran, taken from Reddit's "hot" ranking, which has no time window. Everything is what Reddit users claim unless a line says it was checked. Durable findings are promoted into the Tips tab, which is deduplicated and kept current.

  • Verified checked against primary documentation
  • Reported several independent people claim it
  • Single source one person's claim
  • Unconfirmed looked for a primary source and found none
